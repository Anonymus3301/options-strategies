Strategy Explained

Construction: buy 1 near-ATM call (K1), sell 1 further-OTM call (K2), sell 1 even-further-OTM call (K3) — three strikes total.

Market view: bullish, but expecting a moderate rally rather than an explosive one.

Why trade it: selling the second call finances the trade further than a plain bull call spread would, lowering the cost (or even producing a net credit).

Max profit: a plateau between K2 and K3, equal to (K2 − K1) − net cost. Max loss: unlimited above K3, since the position becomes a net naked short call there.

Key risk: the very thing that makes this cheap (selling an extra call) is what removes the cap on the upside — this is a bet on a moderate move, not a home run.
