Strategy Explained

Construction: put-call parity (C − P = S − K at r=0) implies a spot price at every single strike. The rest of this app aggregates all of them into one robust median "fair" spot (qImpliedSpot); this page keeps every strike's own implied spot separate and ranks them by how far each one deviates from that median.

Market view: none directional — this is an internal pricing-consistency check across the chain, not a bet on where BTC goes. A strike whose call is priced rich relative to its put (vs. the chain's consensus) points to a Conversion (long spot + short call + long put); the reverse points to a Reversal (short spot + long call + short put).

Why it matters: the Box Spread page checks parity at one strike pair you pick; a real dislocation could be sitting at any strike or expiry. This scans all of them and surfaces the largest one first.

Max profit / max loss: in principle a locked-in edge at expiry (same idea as the Box Spread and Jelly Roll pages) — but only if the reference "fair" spot (the chain's own median) turns out to be right; a genuinely mispriced whole chain would show a real edge here that isn't actually capturable.

Key risk / caveat: the "fair" spot is itself derived from the same chain being scanned — this is a relative/internal-consistency signal, not a check against an independent spot or futures price. A large edge more often means a stale or thin quote at that one strike than a genuine, capturable dislocation; always framed as a pricing-consistency signal, the same honesty caveat already attached to the Jelly Roll page.
