Strategy Explained

Construction: buy an at-the-money call and an at-the-money put at the same strike and expiry (a long straddle).

Market view: direction-agnostic — this expects a big move in either direction, pure long gamma and long vega.

Why trade it: profits when the realized move (or a repricing higher of IV) exceeds what was priced in at entry. Favored here when IV Rank is low, IV sits below realized vol, and the expiry is short-dated for cheap convexity per dollar.

Max profit: unlimited above, large (bounded only by BTC reaching zero) below. Max loss: the total premium paid, if price pins exactly at the strike through expiry. Breakeven: strike minus total premium, and strike plus total premium.

Key risk: time decay works against this position every day nothing happens — a straddle needs a real move, not just any move, to overcome its cost.
