Strategy Explained

Construction: sell 1 OTM put on margin — unlike the Income Scanner's cash-secured put, this doesn't assume the full notional is set aside in cash to cover assignment, so it's more capital-efficient but carries real leverage risk if margin requirements change or price falls hard.

Market view: neutral-to-bullish — profits as long as price stays above the strike, without needing a rally, and (unlike the cash-secured version) without tying up the full purchase price to do it.

Why (some) traders use it: the same premium as a cash-secured put for a fraction of the capital committed — the tradeoff is that a sharp decline can produce a margin call or forced liquidation before the position would otherwise have recovered.

Max profit: the premium collected, kept if price finishes at or above the strike. Max loss: large — bounded only by BTC reaching zero, same worst case as a cash-secured put, but reached on less collateral. Breakeven: strike − premium collected.

Key risk: margin, not just price — a large enough adverse move can force liquidation at a worse price than simply holding the assignment to expiry would have, a risk the cash-secured version of this trade doesn't carry.
