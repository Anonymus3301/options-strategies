Strategy Explained

Construction: a weighted ATM straddle — Strap: 2 long calls + 1 long put (bullish lean); Strip: 1 long call + 2 long puts (bearish lean).

Market view: long volatility with a directional tilt — expects a big move, and leans toward believing it's more likely in one particular direction.

Why trade it: costs more than a plain straddle (three legs instead of two) but pays off more if the favored direction is the one that actually happens.

Max loss: the total premium paid across all three legs, if price pins at the strike. Max profit: larger on the favored side than a plain straddle would give, smaller (but still profitable on a big enough move) on the other side.

Key risk: needs a bigger move to overcome the extra cost vs. a plain straddle; a big move in the "wrong" direction pays off less than a plain straddle would have.
