Strategy Explained

Construction: buy 1 near-ATM put (K1), sell 1 further-OTM put (K2), sell 1 even-further-OTM put (K3) — the bearish mirror of the Call Ladder.

Market view: bearish, expecting a moderate decline rather than a crash.

Why trade it: selling the second put finances the trade further than a plain bear put spread would, lowering the cost.

Max profit: a plateau between K3 and K2. Max loss: severe (floor-bounded at zero) below K3, since the position becomes net short an extra put there.

Key risk: the moderate-decline thesis breaks down badly on a crash — exactly the scenario a plain protective structure would want to be prepared for.
