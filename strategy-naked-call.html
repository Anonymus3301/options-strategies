Strategy Explained

Construction: sell 1 OTM call, with nothing behind it — no held BTC (unlike a covered call) and no further-OTM long call for protection (unlike a Bear Call Spread).

Market view: neutral-to-bearish — profits as long as price stays below the strike, without needing a decline.

Why (some) traders use it: collects the largest possible credit for a given strike, since there's no protective leg eating into the premium — the tradeoff is carrying the position's full, uncapped tail risk alone rather than sharing it with a hedge.

Max profit: the premium collected, kept if price finishes at or below the strike. Max loss: genuinely unlimited — there is no strike above which losses stop growing. Breakeven: strike + premium collected.

Key risk: this is the highest-risk-per-dollar-of-margin structure on this site — a large, fast rally can produce a loss many multiples of the premium collected, with no built-in floor on how bad it can get.
