Not financial advice. "Net Roll Cash Flow" is the combined cost of closing the front leg and opening the back leg right now at quoted mark prices — it isn't a guaranteed fill, ignores fees and bid/ask spread, and doesn't account for early assignment risk on the leg being closed. Rolling to a new strike changes your position's risk shape as well as its expiry; this only prices the transaction, not the resulting position's own greeks (see the Portfolio Greeks Dashboard for that).
