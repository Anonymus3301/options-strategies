Payoff at Front Expiry

Front leg settles at intrinsic; the still-alive back leg is repriced via Black-Scholes at its current IV

Not financial advice. Like the Calendar Spread page, the back leg's value at front expiry assumes its current IV holds — a simplification, since IV can move by then. This diagonal adds directional exposure on top of that: it profits most if BTC drifts up toward the short strike by front expiry while IV cooperates.
