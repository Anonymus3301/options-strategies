Reference

The identity: at r=0, C − P = S − K for any strike. Rearranged six ways, this gives every position an exact synthetic build: Long Call = Long Stock + Long Put; Short Call = Short Stock + Short Put; Long Put = Short Stock + Long Call; Short Put = Long Stock + Short Call; Long Stock = Long Call + Short Put (the Synthetic Forward page's own construction); Short Stock = Short Call + Long Put.

Why it matters: recognizing synthetic equivalence explains why a Protective Put (long stock + long put) has the same shape as a plain long call, why a Covered Call (long stock + short call) has the same shape as a short put, and why the Box Spread and Jelly Roll pages' arbitrage checks are really just this same identity applied twice.

Stock convention: "stock" here means an assumed 1 BTC position priced at the implied spot, the same convention as the Protective Put, Collar, and Covered Put pages — not a fetched real position.
