Not financial advice. Annualized yield is a simple premium/time extrapolation, not a compounded or risk-adjusted return, and assumes you can repeat the trade every cycle at the same terms (unlikely). Covered calls cap your upside if BTC rallies past the strike; cash-secured puts obligate you to buy BTC at the strike if it falls below it. Delta is a Black-Scholes estimate from each strike's own quoted IV, not a guarantee of assignment probability.
