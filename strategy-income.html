Strategy Explained

Construction: for holders — sell an OTM call against BTC you already hold (covered call), or sell an OTM cash-secured put if you're willing to buy BTC lower (CSP). This page scans every live expiry and strike in a selectable delta band and ranks both by annualized premium yield.

Market view: neutral-to-mildly-bullish for a covered call; neutral-to-mildly-bearish (willing to buy the dip) for a CSP.

Why trade it: harvest the option's time decay as recurring income against a position you already want to hold, rather than as a standalone directional bet.

Max profit: covered call — (strike − cost basis) + premium, if assigned. CSP — the premium collected, if price stays above the strike. Max loss: covered call — the same downside as holding BTC outright, minus the premium cushion. CSP — owning BTC at (strike − premium), even if price has fallen well below that by expiry.

Key risk: a covered call caps upside entirely above the strike if assigned; a CSP obligates a purchase at the strike no matter how far the market has fallen by then.

Not financial advice. Annualized yield is a simple premium/time extrapolation, not a compounded or risk-adjusted return, and assumes you can repeat the trade every cycle at the same terms (unlikely). Covered calls cap your upside if BTC rallies past the strike; cash-secured puts obligate you to buy BTC at the strike if it falls below it. Delta is a Black-Scholes estimate from each strike's own quoted IV, not a guarantee of assignment probability. Ignores fees, early exercise (American- style is not how these are settled on Deribit, but venues vary), and taxes.
