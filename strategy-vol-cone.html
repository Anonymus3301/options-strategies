Strategy Explained

Construction: the classic "volatility cone" (Burghardt-style): for each window length (7/14/30/60/90 days), computes realized vol on every rolling window of that length across ~400 days of real daily closes, then plots today's reading against the min/25th/median/75th/max of that whole historical distribution.

Market view: none by itself — a measurement, not a position. Every other page here that mentions realized vol (Premium Selling, Long Volatility, the Strategy Hub snapshot) shows one current number per window; this page is the distribution that number sits inside.

Why it matters: "30D RVol is 55%" means very different things if 55% is near the bottom of its usual range versus near the top. This page answers that directly with a real historical percentile, computed from actual price history rather than a short, browser-local accumulated sample the way the Skew/Convexity Rank pages' history does.

How to use it: cross-reference with the IV-vs-RVol comparisons on Premium Selling and Long Volatility — a current RVol reading sitting near the top of its own historical cone is a different, more informative signal than the same absolute number sitting near the bottom.

Key risk / caveat: ~400 days of daily closes for BTC still isn't a long sample by traditional-market standards, and crypto's realized vol regime can shift structurally (exchange changes, macro regime shifts) in ways a historical cone can't anticipate.
