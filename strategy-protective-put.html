Protection floor

Strategy Explained

Construction: for holders — buy an OTM put below spot as portfolio insurance against a held BTC position.

Market view: still bullish or neutral long-term, but wants to cap downside risk over the option's life.

Why trade it: protects against a crash while retaining full upside above the floor, minus the cost of the put — the options equivalent of buying insurance on an asset you plan to keep holding.

Max loss: capped at (spot − floor strike) + premium paid, no matter how far price falls. Max profit: unlimited upside, reduced by the cost of the put. Breakeven: spot plus the premium paid, on the upside.

Key risk: the put's cost recurs every time it's rolled to a new expiry — over many periods, that insurance premium is a real, ongoing drag on returns, just like any recurring insurance policy.
