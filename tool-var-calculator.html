Not financial advice. VaR is the loss level you'd expect to exceed only 5% (or 1%) of the time under the risk-neutral lognormal distribution implied by the enabled legs' average IV — the same driftless-in-log convention behind every Probability of Profit stat on this site, not a real-world/objective forecast. CVaR (Expected Shortfall) is the average loss given you're already past that threshold — a defined-risk structure's CVaR is capped at its max loss; an undefined-risk one (a naked short leg) is not. Ignores fees, margin, and early assignment.
