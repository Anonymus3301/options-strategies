Covered Call Overwrite (2:1 Ratio Write)

Sell 2 OTM calls per 1 BTC held instead of 1 — doubles the income below the strike, but flips a plain covered call's flat, safe upside into a genuinely unlimited-loss short position above it

Strategy Explained

Construction: for an assumed 1 BTC long position — instead of a plain covered call (sell 1 OTM call), sell 2 OTM calls at the same strike. The extra short call is naked against the held BTC.

Market view: neutral-to-mildly-bullish up to the strike, and a conviction that price will not run meaningfully past it — the opposite of a plain covered call, which is comfortable being wrong about that.

Why trade it: the extra premium roughly doubles the income of a plain covered call for the same strike, and below the strike the payoff is simply that covered call with a bigger cushion.

Max profit: (strike − spot) + 2×premium, achieved exactly at the strike. Max loss: unlimited above the strike — this is the critical difference from a plain covered call.

Key risk: a plain covered call is flat (0 slope) above its strike — you keep the stock's worth up to the cap and no more, but never lose more from a further rally. A 2:1 ratio write's slope above the strike is −1: past the "unlimited-risk breakeven" shown below, every further dollar BTC rises costs you a dollar, with no ceiling. This is NOT simply "a covered call with a lower cap" — it is a fundamentally more dangerous, undefined-risk position that happens to look similar below the strike.
