Strategy Explained

Construction: Phase 1 — sell an OTM cash-secured put. If price stays above the strike, keep the premium and repeat. If assigned, you now own BTC at (strike − premium). Phase 2 — sell an OTM covered call against that BTC. If it expires worthless, keep the premium and sell another; if called away, you're back to cash and the wheel starts over at Phase 1.

Market view: neutral-to-mildly-bullish; comfortable owning BTC at the put strike and comfortable having it called away at the call strike — the wheel is a way to get paid while waiting for either to happen.

Why trade it: a single, repeatable income process instead of separately deciding "CSP or covered call" — you're always doing whichever one matches the BTC you currently hold (cash or spot).

What this page shows: Phase 1 is a live, real scan — the same delta-band/annualized-yield methodology as the Income Scanner page, so the two agree. Phase 2 is necessarily hypothetical: it can't know whether Phase 1 will actually be assigned, so it shows what a covered call sold at the assumed cost basis would look like right now, using the next listed expiry after the CSP's.

Key risk: a sustained drop can assign the put well above where BTC then trades, locking in a cost basis above market — and a sustained rally after that can cap the eventual covered call's upside, meaning the wheel underperforms simply holding BTC in strong trending markets either way. The "Illustrated Full-Cycle Yield" below is a simple average of two separate premiums, not a compounded or realized return.
