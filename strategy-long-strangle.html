Strategy Explained

Construction: buy an OTM call above spot and an OTM put below spot, both at a selectable width.

Market view: same as Long Volatility's straddle — expects a big move either direction — but cheaper and requiring a bigger move to pay off.

Why trade it: costs less than an ATM straddle since both legs start out-of-the-money, useful when a trader wants long-vol exposure at lower cost and accepts lower odds of a payout.

Max loss: total premium paid, if price stays between the two strikes through expiry. Max profit: unlimited above, large (bounded at zero) below. Breakeven: put strike minus premium, and call strike plus premium.

Key risk: the wider the strikes chosen, the cheaper the trade but the lower the probability of ever reaching breakeven — a direct cost-vs-probability tradeoff.
