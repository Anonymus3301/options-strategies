Strategy Explained

Construction: sell 1 near-ATM put, buy 2 puts at a further OTM (lower) strike — the bearish mirror of the Call Backspread.

Market view: wants either a sharp decline (max profit territory) or a bounce back above the short strike; the worst outcome is a moderate decline that stalls exactly at the long strike.

Why trade it: usually cheap or a small credit, offering a defined max loss with large (floor-bounded) profit potential on a crash.

Max loss: capped, occurring if price finishes exactly at the long strike. Max profit: large, growing the further price falls below the long strike, bounded only by BTC's floor at zero.

Key risk: at identical strikes, this position is the exact portfolio negation of a Put Ratio Spread — every dollar one makes, the other loses — so pick the side matching your actual view: bearish-with-a-floor here, or mildly-bearish-with-tail-risk there.
