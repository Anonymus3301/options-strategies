Strategy Explained

Construction: buy (long) or sell (short) one ATM call and one ATM put, same strike, same expiry — the tightest, most expensive version of a volatility bet before Long Strangle widens the strikes out for a cheaper trade, or Guts moves both legs in-the-money for a more expensive one.

Market view (long): a big move is coming, direction unknown — the same view the Long Volatility page scores for using IV Rank and realized-vol comparisons before recommending a buy. Market view (short): price stays range-bound through expiry — the same view the Premium Selling page scores for, though that page's own scanner leans toward a strangle rather than this tighter straddle.

Max loss / profit (long): loss capped at premium paid, profit unlimited. Max loss / profit (short): profit capped at premium received, loss unlimited — see Iron Butterfly for a defined-risk way to sell this same view.

Breakeven: strike ± total premium, in both directions, for either side of the trade.

Key risk: long side needs a move bigger than the ATM strangle would (since it starts paying full ATM premium for both legs); short side has unlimited-loss tail risk exactly like a naked call or put, just centered rather than one-sided.
