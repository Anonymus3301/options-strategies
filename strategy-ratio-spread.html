Strategy Explained

Construction: buy 1 near-ATM call, sell 2 calls at a further OTM strike.

Market view: mildly bullish up to the short strike, but not expecting an explosive move past it.

Why trade it: often enterable for a small debit or even a net credit, since the second short call's premium helps finance (or overfinances) the long call.

Max profit: (short strike − long strike) − net cost, achieved exactly at the short strike. Max loss: unlimited above the upper breakeven, since there's a net naked short call once price is past both strikes.

Key risk: explicitly not a defined-risk strategy despite superficially resembling one — a sharp rally causes uncapped losses, unlike the Iron Condor or Butterfly pages.

Not financial advice — this structure has unlimited risk. Above the upper breakeven, losses grow one-for-one with BTC's price with no cap, exactly like a naked short call. It is not a defined-risk strategy like the Iron Condor or Butterfly pages, even though it can be entered for a net credit. Margin requirements for the naked short leg are not modeled here at all.
