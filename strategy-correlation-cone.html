Strategy Explained

Construction: the same volatility-cone idea as the Realized Volatility Cone page, applied to cross-asset correlation instead of single-asset vol — for each window length (14/30/60/90 days), computes BTC/ETH log-return correlation on every rolling window of that length across ~400 days of real daily closes, then plots today's reading against that distribution's min/25th/median/75th/max.

Market view: none by itself — a measurement, not a position. The BTC/ETH Vol Pair page shows today's 30D correlation as a single number; this page is the historical distribution that number sits inside.

Why it matters: BTC/ETH correlation is not constant — it compresses and decompresses with the market cycle. A reading of "85%" means something different if that's near the bottom of its usual range versus the top, the same logic as the Volatility Cone applied to a different underlying metric.

How to use it: cross-reference with the BTC/ETH Vol Pair page — a correlation reading sitting at a historical extreme is a more informative signal for that page's relative-value vol trade than the same absolute number without that context.

Key risk / caveat: ~400 days is still a limited sample, and BTC/ETH correlation can shift with structural changes in the market (new correlated products, macro regime shifts) that a historical cone can't anticipate.
