Strategy Explained

Construction: sell a ~25Δ put, plus a call credit spread (sell a ~20Δ call, buy a further-OTM call).

Market view: neutral-to-bullish — wants price to stay above the short put and below the short call.

Why trade it: if the total credit collected covers the width of the call spread, there is no risk at all on the upside — a defining feature of this structure — leaving only the downside to manage, much like a naked put.

Max profit: the total credit collected, if price finishes between the short put and short call at expiry. Max loss: uncapped on the downside (bounded only by BTC reaching zero); capped on the upside at (call spread width − credit), which is zero or negative — i.e., no risk — if credit ≥ width.

Key risk: "no upside risk" only refers to the call side; the short put remains fully exposed to a crash, just like a standalone naked put.
