Not financial advice. The Taylor estimate uses only delta, gamma, and theta — it ignores vega entirely (this scenario holds IV fixed) and higher-order terms (speed, color), so its error grows with move size even before any real-world IV change is considered. A small move (a percent or two) should show a small error; a large one will show gamma's second-order approximation visibly diverging from the actual repriced value. A teaching/reference tool, not a suggested position.
