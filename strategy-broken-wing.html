Strategy Explained

Construction: like a Butterfly Spread, but the inner and outer wing widths are independently selectable, making it asymmetric.

Market view: a neutral-to-directional pinning bet, skewed toward whichever side has the narrower wing.

Why trade it: when the outer wing is wider than the inner one, this is often financed for a net credit — which can (but doesn't automatically) eliminate risk entirely on one side.

Max profit: at the center strike, same formula as a regular butterfly. Max loss: reported separately below the lower wing and above the upper wing, since — unlike a symmetric butterfly — these two flat regions are usually different values.

Key risk: a net credit does not automatically mean one side is risk-free; this page checks and reports both flat regions honestly rather than assuming so.
