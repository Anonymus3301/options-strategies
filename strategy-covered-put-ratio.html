Covered Put Overwrite (2:1 Ratio Write)

Sell 2 OTM puts per 1 BTC short instead of 1 — the bearish mirror of the Covered Call Overwrite, adding growing loss below the strike to a short position's existing unlimited upside risk

Strategy Explained

Construction: for an assumed 1 BTC short position (e.g. via a dated future) — instead of a plain covered put (sell 1 OTM put), sell 2 OTM puts at the same strike. The extra short put is naked against the short.

Market view: bearish-to-neutral down to the strike, with conviction that price will not keep falling meaningfully past it — the opposite of a plain covered put, which is comfortable being wrong about that.

Why trade it: the extra premium roughly doubles the income of a plain covered put for the same strike, and above the strike the payoff is identical to a plain covered put (unwound there in effect).

Max profit: (spot − strike) + 2×premium, achieved exactly at the strike. Max loss: unlimited above the strike (unchanged from a plain covered put — that risk was already there from the short) plus now-growing loss below the strike as price keeps falling, capped only by BTC's floor at zero.

Key risk: a plain covered put is flat (0 slope) below its strike — the short is effectively closed out there and a further crash costs nothing extra. A 2:1 ratio write's slope below the strike is +1: past the "amplified-risk breakeven" shown below, every further dollar BTC falls costs you a dollar on top of what a plain covered put would have lost. This turns an already undefined-risk short position into one with growing risk on both sides, not just the short's usual upside exposure.

Ignores fees, margin for the naked leg, and early assignment.
