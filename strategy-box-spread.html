Not financial advice, and not risk-free in practice. A box spread's payoff is fixed only in theory — European settlement, no counterparty/margin risk, and the ability to actually trade all four legs at the quoted mark prices are all idealizations. This page reports what the chain's current mark prices imply, not an executable rate. Cross-check against the Carry & Funding page's futures basis — in an efficient market the two should broadly agree.
