Not financial advice. This is arithmetic, not risk management advice — it doesn't know your total portfolio, correlation with other positions, or margin rules on your actual exchange (which can differ from a simple "max loss" figure, especially for undefined-risk structures like the Ratio Spread or Jade Lizard pages). The Kelly estimate is especially sensitive to your win-probability guess being right, which it usually isn't — most practitioners use a fraction (e.g. 25-50%) of the computed Kelly size, not the full amount.
