Strategy Explained

Construction: buy 1 near-ATM put, sell 2 puts at a further OTM (lower) strike — the bearish mirror of the Call Ratio Spread.

Market view: mildly bearish down to the short strike, but not expecting a crash past it.

Why trade it: like its call-side counterpart, often enterable for a small debit or credit since the second short put helps finance the long one.

Max profit: (long strike − short strike) − net cost, achieved exactly at the short strike. Max loss: severe (though technically floor-bounded at BTC = 0) below the lower breakeven — for any realistic position size, this behaves like unlimited downside risk.

Key risk: the bearish equivalent of the Call Ratio Spread's uncapped risk, just facing the other direction; not a defined-risk strategy.
