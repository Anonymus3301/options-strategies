Strategy Explained

Construction: sell 1 near-ATM (or slightly OTM) put, buy 1 put at a further OTM strike for protection, same expiry — a defined-risk credit spread, the capped cousin of a naked short (cash-secured) put.

Market view: neutral-to-bullish — profits as long as price stays above the short strike through expiry, without needing a rally.

Why trade it: collects premium from a bullish-to-neutral view while capping the tail risk a naked short put would carry, at the cost of a lower credit than selling the put alone.

Max profit: the net credit, kept if price finishes at or above the short strike. Max loss: (short strike − long strike) − net credit, at or below the long strike. Breakeven: short strike − net credit.

Key risk: a decline past the long strike hits the full defined max loss — capped, but still a real, sizable loss relative to the credit collected.
