Not financial advice. Unlike the Iron Condor's flat-top profit zone, an Iron Butterfly's max profit only occurs exactly at the center strike — most outcomes land somewhere in between max profit and max loss. Center strike is the nearest listed strike to spot, so the position may not be perfectly symmetric. Wings are rounded to the nearest listed strike on each side.
