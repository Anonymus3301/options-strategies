Strategy Explained

Construction: sell the ATM call and put (the straddle), buy protective wings further out on both sides.

Market view: neutral, same as the Iron Condor, but a tighter, more concentrated bet that price finishes very close to a single strike.

Why trade it: collects more premium than an Iron Condor (since the short strikes are ATM instead of ~20Δ) in exchange for a narrower profit zone.

Max profit: the net credit, achieved only if price finishes exactly at the center strike — a single peak, not a flat plateau like an Iron Condor. Max loss: capped at (wing width − net credit) on either side.

Key risk: most outcomes land somewhere between max profit and max loss; hitting the exact center strike at expiry is a low-probability, high-payout outcome.

Unlike the Iron Condor's flat-top profit zone, an Iron Butterfly's max profit only occurs exactly at the center strike — most outcomes land somewhere in between max profit and max loss. Center strike is the nearest listed strike to spot, so the position may not be perfectly symmetric. Wings are rounded to the nearest listed strike on each side.
