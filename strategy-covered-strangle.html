Strategy Explained

Construction: for holders — sell an OTM call and an OTM put against a held BTC position.

Market view: neutral-to-mildly bullish; comfortable both capping upside and buying more BTC lower if it dips.

Why trade it: collects two premiums instead of one compared to a plain covered call, for a higher yield.

Max profit: capped at (call strike − spot) + total credit, the same ceiling as a covered call. Max loss: below the put strike, losses grow twice as fast as simply holding BTC, since the short put obligates effectively buying more at that price on top of what's already held.

Key risk: explicitly not a hedge, despite superficially resembling one — the extra premium is compensation for taking on additional downside exposure, not protection from it.

The premium collected softens that, but does not remove it. Above the call strike, upside is given up entirely if assigned, just like a covered call.
