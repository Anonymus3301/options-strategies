Long BTC + short call + short put vs. long BTC alone, at expiry

Not financial advice. This is not a hedge — below the put strike, this position loses money twice as fast as simply holding BTC, because the short put obliges you to effectively buy more at that price on top of the BTC you already hold. The premium collected softens that, but does not remove it. Above the call strike, upside is given up entirely if assigned, just like a covered call.
