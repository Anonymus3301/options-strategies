Both greeks use this site's standing r=0, q=0 Black-Scholes convention and were checked against a finite-difference numerical derivative of the site's own delta function before shipping. A delta-hedged position with non-trivial Vanna or Charm needs re-hedging even with price flat — from an IV move (Vanna) or from time simply passing (Charm) — something the Portfolio Greeks Dashboard's static delta/gamma/vega/theta snapshot alone doesn't surface.
