Strategy Explained

Construction: buy 1 put at K4 (highest), sell 1 put at K3, sell 1 put at K2, buy 1 put at K1 (lowest) — the same four strikes as the Call Condor, built entirely from puts instead.

Market view: neutral — identical to the Call Condor's view that price finishes between K2 and K3, since the two structures share the exact same payoff shape at expiry.

Why trade it: functionally interchangeable with the Call Condor at the same strikes (verified numerically: the two produce identical payoffs at every price point) — which one is actually cheaper to put on in practice comes down to the specific bid/ask and skew on the day, not the underlying structure.

Max profit: flat across the whole body (between K2 and K3), equal to (K3 − K4) − net debit... same magnitude as the Call Condor's formula, just built from the put side. Max loss: the net debit paid, identical on both tails when wings are equal width.

Key risk: same as the Call Condor — the wider profit zone costs more than a tighter Butterfly-style structure would.
