The debit mirror of the Iron Condor: a flat loss zone in the middle instead of a flat profit plateau, capped gains outside the outer strikes

Not financial advice. Maximum loss (the full debit paid) happens if price pins anywhere between the two inner strikes at expiry — the most likely single outcome for most assets most of the time, which is exactly why this structure needs a real move to pay off. The outer strikes cap the maximum profit; without them this would just be a long strangle.
