Not financial advice. This page assumes a short BTC notional (for example via a dated future or the perpetual) the same way the Protective Put and Collar pages assume a held long position — it does not fetch or account for an actual futures position, funding costs, or margin. Profit is capped once price falls to the put strike, since the position is unwound there in effect; if price rises instead, losses are unlimited, same as any naked short.
