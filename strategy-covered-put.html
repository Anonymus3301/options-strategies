Strategy Explained

Construction: for an assumed short BTC position — sell an OTM put for extra income. The bearish mirror of a covered call.

Market view: bearish-to-neutral; comfortable buying back the short position at the put strike if price falls that far.

Why trade it: collects premium against a short position the same way a covered call does against a long one.

Max profit: capped at (spot − put strike) + premium, achieved at or below the put strike. Max loss: unlimited if price rises, exactly like any naked short position.

Key risk: this assumes a short notional the same way the Protective Put and Collar pages assume a held long position — it does not fetch or model an actual futures position, funding costs, or margin; the premium only provides a small cushion, not real protection.
