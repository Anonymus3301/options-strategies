Strategy Explained

Construction: sell 1 near-ATM (or slightly OTM) call, buy 1 call at a further OTM strike for protection, same expiry — a defined-risk credit spread, the capped-loss cousin of a naked short call.

Market view: neutral-to-bearish — profits as long as price stays below the short strike through expiry, without needing a decline.

Why trade it: collects premium from a bearish-to-neutral view while capping the tail risk a naked short call would carry, at the cost of a lower credit than selling the call alone.

Max profit: the net credit, kept if price finishes at or below the short strike. Max loss: (long strike − short strike) − net credit, at or above the long strike. Breakeven: short strike + net credit.

Key risk: a rally past the long strike hits the full defined max loss — this is a capped-risk trade, but the cap can still be a meaningful loss relative to the credit collected.
