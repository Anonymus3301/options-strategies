Strategy Explained

Construction: for holders — buy an OTM put (the floor) financed by selling an OTM call (the cap), with strikes picked so the two premiums roughly offset for near-zero net cost.

Market view: neutral-to-mildly-bullish; willing to give up upside beyond the cap in exchange for a floor that costs little or nothing.

Why trade it: a cheaper way to hedge than a naked protective put, at the cost of also capping the upside — useful when protecting a position matters more than preserving unlimited gains.

Max loss: capped at (spot − floor) + net cost. Max profit: capped at (cap − spot) − net cost (or plus net credit, if the position was entered for a credit).

Key risk: if BTC rallies hard, all upside past the cap strike is given up entirely, not just reduced, if held through expiry and assigned.

The call strike above is chosen to roughly offset the put's premium (a "zero-cost" collar), not to hit any particular cap level — if you want a specific cap, you'll pay or receive a net premium either way. Being short the call means any upside past the cap is given up entirely, not just reduced, if you hold through expiry and get assigned.
