Strategy Explained

Construction: sell 1 near-ATM call, buy 2 calls at a further OTM strike.

Market view: wants either a big rally (max profit territory) or a return to well below the short strike; the worst outcome is a moderate rally that stalls right at the long strike.

Why trade it: usually enterable near zero cost (or a small credit), offering a defined, capped max loss with theoretically unlimited profit if price runs far enough.

Max loss: capped, occurring if price finishes exactly at the long strike. Max profit: unlimited above the upside breakeven.

Key risk: the worst-case outcome happens at a specific price level, not from a huge move against you — a backspread can lose money even while being "right" about direction, if the move isn't big enough.

Not financial advice. This is a long-gamma, typically long-vega structure — it benefits from a large move and/or rising IV, and its worst case (a defined, capped loss) happens if price sits exactly at the long strike at expiry, not from a big move against you. Often entered near breakeven cost when skew/IV favor it; check the actual net cost above before assuming it's free.
