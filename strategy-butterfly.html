Strategy Explained

Construction: buy 1 lower-strike call, sell 2 center-strike calls, buy 1 upper-strike call, all at the same expiry (a put butterfly at the same strikes has the same payoff shape).

Market view: neutral — a low-volatility "pinning" bet that price finishes very close to the center strike.

Why trade it: a cheap, defined-risk way to bet on low realized volatility, or on a specific pin level such as ATM or the max-pain strike.

Max profit: (center − lower strike) − net debit, achieved only if price finishes exactly at the center strike. Max loss: capped at the net debit paid — also the most likely single outcome, which is why butterflies are cheap.

Key risk: low probability of hitting max profit exactly; most outcomes land somewhere between max profit and a full loss of the premium paid.
