Strategy Explained

Construction: not a fixed options structure — a relative-value read comparing front-month ATM IV in BTC vs. ETH, their realized correlation, and the vol spread's history, with a suggested pair structure (e.g., long options in the cheaper-vol asset, short in the richer one).

Market view: a bet on ETH's IV richening or cheapening relative to BTC's, not on either asset's own direction.

Why trade it: when the vol spread between the two sits at an extreme vs. its own recent history, it may mean-revert toward its typical level.

Max profit / max loss: depends entirely on the specific structure actually built, not calculated on this page — the figures shown here are the spread and its rank only.

Key risk: named a "pair trade," explicitly not true dispersion (that needs 3+ constituents, which doesn't exist in crypto); the correlation between BTC and ETH can itself shift, breaking the relative-value thesis this trade depends on.
